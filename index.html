I / V

The first day
I saw you

I still remember that day. The first day of college.

You were sitting in the next row to me.

And you — how beautiful you were.

Your dimples.

That was the first time I saw you, and I was already in love with you.

I can still see you sitting there. I can still smell that particular smell of you.

I spent so much time trying to make you mine. So, so much time.

And then I did so many things wrong.

II / V

The thought
that broke me

I was just sitting. And a thought came from nowhere.

You. With someone else.

I can't even have that thought. I can't think of you with someone else.

It sends shivers through me. I don't know what happened to me.

It broke me.

I tried to picture you in someone else's life.

It made me realise something.

I can only find comfort with you.

And I don't know how to say sorry. But I'm going to try.
